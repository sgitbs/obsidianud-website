How It Works

Every Great Project Begins With a Great Process.

At Obsidian United Developments Inc., every project follows a proven process built on clear communication, thoughtful planning, professional project management, and exceptional craftsmanship. Whether we're remodeling a kitchen or completing a large-scale construction project, our goal is to deliver a seamless experience from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

Built on Experience. Driven by Quality.

For more than 39 years, our approach has remained the same.

Listen carefully. Plan thoroughly.
Build with precision. Finish with pride.

Every project deserves the same level of professionalism, attention to detail, and craftsmanship regardless of size or complexity. Our process has been refined through decades of hands-on experience and is designed to give every client confidence throughout every phase of construction.

01

Consultation

Every successful project begins with understanding your vision. During our consultation, we evaluate the space, discuss your goals, answer questions, and identify opportunities that align with your budget and long-term plans. Our focus is to provide honest recommendations and practical solutions before construction ever begins.

02

Detailed Proposal

Following the consultation, we prepare a comprehensive proposal outlining the project scope, recommended materials, estimated timeline, and transparent pricing. We believe every client should understand exactly what to expect before making an investment. No surprises. No hidden costs. Just honest communication.

03

Design & Planning

Thoughtful planning creates exceptional results. Before construction begins, we coordinate layouts, finishes, materials, scheduling, and project logistics to help ensure a smooth and organized construction process. Every detail is considered before work begins.

04

Permits & Preparation

When required, we coordinate the permitting process and prepare every aspect of the project for construction. Our goal is to eliminate unnecessary stress while ensuring work is completed professionally and in accordance with applicable requirements.

05

Construction

This is where planning becomes reality. Our team focuses on craftsmanship, precision, cleanliness, communication, and quality throughout every phase of construction. Every finish is completed with the same attention to detail that has defined our company for nearly four decades.

06

Final Walkthrough

Before every project is considered complete, we perform a comprehensive walkthrough to verify that every detail meets our standards and your expectations. We don't consider a project finished until you are completely satisfied with the final result.
