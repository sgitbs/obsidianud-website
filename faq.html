Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to what clients ask us most before starting a project.

Are you a licensed contractor?+

Yes. Obsidian United Developments holds California Class B General Contractor license #1084732, and we're bonded and insured on every project.

How do you price projects?+

We provide a written, itemized estimate before any work begins — not a rough ballpark. You'll know the full scope and cost of your project before signing anything.

Do you handle permits?+

Yes, we manage the permitting process directly with the city on every project that requires it, including additions, ADUs, and structural work.

How long does a typical remodel take?+

Timelines vary by scope — a bathroom remodel might take 3-4 weeks, while a whole-home remodel or ADU can take several months. We'll give you a realistic schedule during the estimate phase.

Do you offer financing?+

We can point you toward financing options for qualified projects. Reach out and we'll walk you through what's available.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Orange County, Riverside County, the Inland Empire, Los Angeles County, and the surrounding Southern California region. See our Service Areas page for details.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?+

Yes, we stand behind our craftsmanship. Specific warranty terms are outlined in your project proposal and vary by scope of work.

Can I see examples of your past work?+

Absolutely — visit our Portfolio and Before & After pages to see completed projects, or ask us for references during your consultation.
