Overview

What's included

Interior remodeling covers everything that isn't a full kitchen or bathroom gut — opening up a floor plan, upgrading finishes throughout, or reworking a single room. Obsidian United scopes interior work with the same estimate discipline as any larger project, so smaller jobs don't get treated as an afterthought.

  • ◆ Layout and floor plan changes
  • ◆ Wall removal (load-bearing and non-load-bearing)
  • ◆ Finish carpentry and trim work
  • ◆ Lighting and electrical upgrades
  • ◆ Drywall, paint, and flooring coordination
  • ◆ Built-ins and custom millwork
